== filter md plain text for the atmosphere == endfilter == filter md ## Overview **NeoDoc** is a plain-text format for creating, storing, and querying content. It's easy to write without sacrificing the power to make full-featured posts and pages. Basically, it's _markdown_ on steroids with a free-form database thrown in for good measure. NeoDoc provides a clean separation of concerns between layouts and content. Individual apps are in complete control over what they render. Everything becomes extremely portable as a result. Even better, the content is organized into sections with optional attributes. The combination provides the foundation of a queryable interface. Apps can make custom displays of individual posts or aggregate content from across the atmosphere to provide unique views of the world. == endfilter [#
