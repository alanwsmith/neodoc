Everything published to the style.neodoc lexicon is public. (Using the neodoc format in other lexicons that add permissions/privacy layers is totally acceptable and encouraged.) A specific point about this is that content items whose ``isDraft`` flag is ``true`` are public. It's up to apps to determine if/when/how they are to be shown. There is no enforcement of specific criteria. - Partial content parsing is out of scope. Parsing either completes 100% or an error is throw. Focus on making the error message as clear as possible is a priority.
